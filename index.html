ProofKeep

Every warranty claim that fails was winnable a month earlier. ProofKeep tracks what you own, computes the exact coverage end date for each item, sorts by soonest lapse, and warns you while the claim window is still open.

  • Exact coverage end dates, clamped correctly (Jan 31 + 1 month = Feb 28/29)
  • Claim-window warnings at 30 days out - while a claim still works
  • Soonest lapse first, expired items sink to the bottom
  • No account, no cloud - your list stays in your browser
